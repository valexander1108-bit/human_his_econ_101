# Micro Policy Lab Model Alignment Audit

Source reviewed: `dev_materials/micropolicylab`.

## Lab Model Requirement

The micro policy lab asks students to model one policy problem: positive externalities in education and the subsidy needed to move from the private market outcome to the socially optimal outcome.

Required data-entry fields from the worksheet:

- `PMB = 100 - 5Q`
- `PMC = 10 + 2Q`
- `SMB = 120 - 5Q`
- Quantity is measured as years of schooling.
- Students calculate market equilibrium, social optimum, underprovision, DWL, and corrective subsidy.
