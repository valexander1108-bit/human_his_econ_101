## Timeline Additions

The timeline now includes the 56 scenarios from the course sequence CSV plus supplemental scenarios from Modules 6-12 worksheets, preserving all prior rows. Supplemental rows add historical/worked examples for Hana in Seoul, Malthus/Black Death, Indonesia wellbeing, Detroit/Flint/Pittsburgh/NAFTA firm cases, Iowa corn, AT&T, OPEC, Boeing/Airbus, airline HHI, Midwest chemical plant, tax incidence, mortgage crisis behavior, carbon tax incidence, and a free-rider public-goods case.

All scenario titles were standardized to the original worksheet style, such as "The Farmer of Ur": each title now begins with "The" and identifies an actor or group in a historical place. Every scenario also has a `historical_artifact` entry and a rewritten description that connects the artifact to the model students open from the map.

## Global Perspective Additions

To reduce the U.S. concentration in Modules 9-12, additional past-century scenarios were added for Japan, South Korea, China, Bangladesh, Mexico, Kenya, Southern Africa, India, the European Union, Brazil, Costa Rica, Norway, Peru, and Rwanda. These support cost of production, economies of scale, shutdown, perfect competition, monopoly, price discrimination, antitrust, platform competition, climate policy, positive externalities, public goods, common resources, and behavioral policy.
