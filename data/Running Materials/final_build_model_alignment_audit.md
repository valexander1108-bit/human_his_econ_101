## Removed from student-facing catalog

- `Income and Substitution Effects` was removed from Module 2 because the Final Build worksheet references income/substitution effects only as background for demand, not as a standalone model problem.
- `Competition and Fairness` was removed from Module 7 because the Final Build worksheet keeps Module 7 focused on poverty traps, Lorenz/Gini, monopsony/labor power, and game theory preview. The fairness model remains in Modules 8 and 11 where the worksheets directly address algorithmic fairness and market competition.
